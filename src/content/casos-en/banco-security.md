## In one sentence

I joined Banco Security to code an onboarding that was already designed, and over time I moved on to proposing how that experience should work and building it myself, without waiting for another team to design it.

## Context

The service, provided between 2017 and 2019, was for users who wanted to buy and sell stocks. Before trading, they had to go through an identity-verification (KYC) onboarding: the point where a bank decides whether it trusts who is in front of it.

## How my role changed along the way

This is what sets this case apart from the others: I didn't start by designing. I joined as a **Front End developer**, implementing in **Angular 12** flows that already came designed. As I understood the product and its users better, I started contributing **user experience ideas and proposals**, and I implemented them myself as part of the development team, instead of passing them to another role.

It's the same path I describe in my profile, but walked in the opposite direction: from building what others design, to designing what I then build.

## What I delivered

- **Angular 12 implementation** of the KYC onboarding flow for investments, already in production.
- **Onboarding flow proposals** for the investment site, aimed at reducing friction and user drop-off during the process.
- **Implementation of those proposals**, integrated into the bank's development team.
