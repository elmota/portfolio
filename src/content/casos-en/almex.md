## In one sentence

I designed and built, end to end, the platform where a mining operator reports a fault in their machinery and that report becomes a maintenance job with a certificate, without losing traceability between the field and the system.

## Context

Almex runs inventory and maintenance for mining machinery and equipment —trucks, conveyor trays and other assets. The problem wasn't only about the interface: it was understanding how an operator, often in the field and under demanding working conditions, can report a fault in a way that turns that report into real maintenance action.

## The design problem

A report that doesn't lead to certified maintenance is useless. The platform needed to connect three moments: **detecting and reporting** the problem, **carrying out** the maintenance, and **certifying** that it happened, with language and a flow that a field operator could use without friction.

## Key decisions

1. **The research started with the user, not with a screen.** The problem was defined together with the people who report faults in the field, before proposing any flow.
2. **Three surfaces, one model.** The web app, the desktop app and the system module share the same inventory and maintenance logic, so the data doesn't drift between the person who reports and the person who administers.
3. **End to end, one set of criteria.** By taking the project from research through implementation and launch, UX decisions reached the code without being reinterpreted along the way.

## What I delivered

- **User research** in the real context of fault reporting.
- **User flows and prototypes** for problem reporting, maintenance and certification.
- **Interface design** for the web app, the desktop app and the system.
- **Frontend development** of the solution and its launch.

## Current status

The platform is in production. The maintenance work that continues today is mainly **operational** —about the inventory and maintenance processes themselves, not the frontend code— which suggests the foundation that was built has stayed stable.
