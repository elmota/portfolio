## In one sentence

It wasn't one project, it was four: for almost a decade I worked with Banco de Chile and its brands on different engagements, from selling insurance online to maintaining an internal system for Teletón for six years and programming the bank's payments app.

## Engagement 1 · Insurance portal (2011–2012)

I proposed and designed an insurance portal for customers of Banco de Chile and its brands —Banco Edwards, Banchile—, working directly with the client to understand their needs. I delivered design proposals and prototypes in HTML, CSS and JS.

## Engagement 2 · Internet Explorer 6 compatibility (2012)

I defined and handed to the development team, who worked in Java, HTML specifications focused on making the site work correctly in IE6 —a browser that even then imposed strict limits on what an interface could and couldn't do.

## Engagement 3 · Teletón sign-up system (2012–2018)

An internal project so bank employees could apply to take part in the bank's branches during the Teletón periods, and so branch managers could review and confirm those applications.

I defined the information architecture, the design and the prototypes, and delivered in HTML, CSS and JS with jQuery and KendoUI. I worked directly with the user —the bank's counterpart— both on the initial design and in the later maintenance and improvement phases, which ran through 2018.

It's the longest-running of the four engagements: six years sustaining a single system, not just building it, and **the bank still uses the system to this day**, well beyond the period in which I took part.

## Engagement 4 · Mi Pago app (2020)

Mobile developer on the Mi Pago app team, with integrations in **React Native**. I worked with **Scrum**, as part of a development team and not as an individual engagement.

## Why this case matters

These four engagements show a relationship sustained over time with a single client, across different roles: design for the end customer, technical specification for another team, architecture of an internal system I maintained for years, and integration as a developer within a Scrum team. It's the same versatility I describe in the rest of the portfolio, but with a single brand as the common thread for almost ten years.
